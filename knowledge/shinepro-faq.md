# ShinePro Detailing — Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you come to my house? Do you come to me? Are you a mobile service?
Yes. ShinePro Detailing is a fully mobile detailing service. We come to you — at your
home, your work, or wherever the car is parked. You don't need to bring the car anywhere.

## How long does interior detailing take?
Interior detailing takes about 1 to 2 hours. This is an estimate; the actual time
depends on the condition of the car.

## How long does exterior detailing take?
Exterior detailing takes about 1 to 2 hours. This is an estimate; the actual time
depends on the condition of the car.

## How long does a full detail take? How long does a full interior and exterior detail take?
A full detail (interior and exterior together) takes about 2 to 3 hours. This is an
estimate; the actual time depends on the condition of the car.

## How long does ceramic coating take?
Ceramic coating takes about 4 to 5 hours. This is an estimate; the actual time depends
on the condition of the car.

## How long does paint correction take?
Paint correction takes about 4 to 7 hours. This is an estimate; the actual time depends
on the condition of the car.

## What are your hours?
Appointments are available 8 AM to 5 PM in your own local time.
